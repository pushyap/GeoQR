Revolutionize how you track attendance with GPS-verified QR codes, device authentication, and real-time validation for educational institutions.

Features

Everything You Need for Modern Attendance

Powerful features designed to make attendance tracking secure, efficient, and reliable.

Dynamic QR Codes

Auto-refreshing QR codes that expire in seconds, preventing screenshot-based proxy attendance.

GPS Radius Validation

Verify student's physical presence within a configured radius of the attendance location.

Device Authentication

Secure device registration ensures only authorized QR generators can create valid attendance codes.

Event-Based Attendance

Create sessions for lectures, labs, or events with automatic tracking and management.

Attendance History

Comprehensive logs with timestamps, locations, and statistics for students and administrators.

Secure & Reliable

End-to-end security with JWT authentication, encrypted tokens, and audit trails.

Process

How GeoQR Works

A simple 4-step process to seamlessly track attendance.

1

Admin Sets Location

Administrator configures attendance locations with GPS coordinates and valid radius.

2

Device Generates QR

Authenticated device generates time-limited, encrypted QR codes for the session.

3

Student Scans QR

Student opens the app, scans the QR code displayed in the classroom.

4

Attendance Validated

System validates QR token, GPS location, and records attendance securely.
